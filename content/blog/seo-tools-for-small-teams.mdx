Most SEO software is built for agencies managing dozens of client sites. If you're a small team with one or two properties, that complexity is a tax you don't need to pay.

## Start with what you're actually missing

Before buying anything, audit what you already have. Search Console and a spreadsheet cover more of the fundamentals than most teams assume — indexation status, core web vitals, and top query data are all free.

## When a paid tool earns its keep

Paid tools start paying for themselves once you need automated crawling at scale, competitor content-gap analysis, or prioritized technical fixes ranked by actual traffic impact rather than raw error counts.

## What to avoid

Skip any tool that quotes you enterprise pricing with a soft "let's hop on a call" — for a one- or two-site team, you want transparent monthly pricing and a real free trial, not a sales process.
